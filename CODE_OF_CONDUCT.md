# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in Locked's issues, pull requests, and other project spaces is expected to help maintain a respectful and welcoming community.

- Be considerate of people with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and respect differing views.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal, school, or account information without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or sexually target other participants. Personal attacks and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to uphold these expectations. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report concerns to [@JacobScheff](https://github.com/JacobScheff) through a private contact method listed on the profile. If none is available, request a private contact in an issue without publishing sensitive details. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and protect the privacy of people involved.
